RuneScope is an independent, fan-made market companion for Old School RuneScape. Grand Exchange research, profit calculators, and a trade journal that remembers what you actually did, so that later it can tell you which of your plans were worth following.

It shares its calculations with OSRS Toolkit for Windows, the desktop app it grew out of. Run either, or both: connect the desktop app to your account here and a trade recorded in one shows up in the other.

Game interaction

RuneScope does not play the game. It sends no input, changes no network traffic, and modifies no game client. The optional RuneLite plugin reports trades after they have happened; it never acts.

It never asks for a Jagex password, bank PIN, or authenticator code.

Your data

Your journal lives on the server that runs RuneScope, which is what lets you reach it from any device. Connect the desktop app and it mirrors the same journal both ways. Leave it disconnected and it keeps everything on your PC.

  • What is stored: your trade journal, tracked positions, imported RuneLite events, watchlist, savings goal, and your character names. One database file per account. Tokens are kept as hashes, never as the token itself.
  • Sign-in: a Discord user id, username and avatar. Or, without Discord, just the hash of a recovery code. No passwords, no email addresses.
  • Prices come from the OSRS Wiki real-time price API. Character lookup reads the public hiscores. Neither knows who asked.
  • Taking it with you: the CSV export on your profile is your whole journal, any time.

Where the numbers come from

Prices are completed transactions, so an item can fail to fill at a price it traded at minutes ago. Margins, GP/hour and boss requirements are estimates built from market data and community sources.
