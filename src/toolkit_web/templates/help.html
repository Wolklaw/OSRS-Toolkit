Getting started

  1. Sign in. With Discord, or with a recovery code instead. No password, no email address either way.
  2. Use it straight away. The GE Flipper, Alch Finder, Skilling Profit and Watchlist need nothing from you but a cash stack, they just read live market prices.
  3. Connect the plugin when you want the journal to fill itself in. Without it you can still record trades by hand on the Trade Journal page. With it, your real fills arrive on their own. Installing it takes about five minutes while it waits on Plugin Hub approval.

Connecting the RuneLite plugin

  1. Install the OSRS Toolkit Sync plugin in RuneLite. It's not on the Plugin Hub yet, so for now that means building it yourself: the steps are here, and they take about five minutes.
  2. On your profile, choose Get a token and copy the token it shows you.
  3. In RuneLite, open the plugin's settings and paste it into Pairing token under Connection.
  4. Switch on Send to OSRS Toolkit Sync. The plugin will say in the chatbox whether the token was accepted.
  5. Under What to record, pick what you want sent: Track Grand Exchange fills, Track player trades, and Sync gear and bank for PvM readiness. All three are yours to leave off.
  6. Play. Your fills will appear under RuneLite Activity, and positions open on the Dashboard, the next time a page loads.

For PvM Readiness specifically, turn on Sync gear and bank for PvM readiness and then open your bank in game once. That's the moment the snapshot gets taken.

Connecting the desktop app

Optional, and separate from the plugin above. Download the app first if you haven't already. Connecting it points the Windows app at your account here, so its journal mirrors this one and it can show your live Grand Exchange slots. Leave it disconnected and it just keeps its journal on your PC, like it always has.

  1. On your profile, under Desktop app, choose Get a token and copy it. This is a different credential from the plugin's pairing token above: it's what proves the desktop app belongs to your account, not what feeds the sync service.
  2. In the desktop app, open Settings → Website and paste it in.
  3. Leave the website address alone unless you're running your own copy of RuneScope.
  4. A trade recorded in either place reaches the other within a minute, sooner if the desktop window is the one in front. Deleting a trade in one removes it from both.

Lost a token, or replacing a PC? Revoke the old one from your profile and make a new one. Nothing already synced gets lost.

What each page is for

  • Dashboard: open positions, your live Grand Exchange slots, and net worth over time. This is also where you record what a position actually filled at.
  • GE Flipper: what to buy right now with the cash and free slots you have. "Track" turns a suggestion into a position in your journal.
  • Watchlist: items you want to keep an eye on whether or not they clear the flipper's bar.
  • Alch Finder: what's worth high-alching today, after nature runes and after what it costs to buy.
  • Skilling Profit: gathering and processing methods priced off the live market, with a hiscores lookup to mark what you can actually do.
  • Trade Journal: flips completed outside the plugin, recorded by hand.
  • RuneLite Activity: the raw feed of what the plugin has sent.
  • Buy Limits: how much of each item you can still buy this window.
  • Performance: which strategies and items actually made you money, and where your plans and your results parted ways.
  • PvM Readiness: your gear, bank and levels against boss checklists.
  • Savings Goal: one target, and an honest estimate of how far off it is.

Questions people actually ask

Do I need the plugin?

No. Everything that reads the market, the GE Flipper, Watchlist, Alch Finder and Skilling Profit, works with nothing connected. The plugin just saves you typing: it fills in the journal, the live offer slots, the buy-limit counter and PvM Readiness for you.

Why do prices look out of date?

Two different clocks. The price pill in the header says when this server last fetched the market, press it to fetch again. "Age" on an item says when that item last actually traded, which for a quiet item can be hours ago no matter how fresh the fetch was.

A suggested flip didn't fill at the suggested price.

Prices here are completed transactions, not offers sitting on the book, so the next offer can miss the last one's price. That's what the flipper's confidence figure is for: it weighs volume and how recently the item traded.

How does the GE tax get counted?

Two percent of the sale price, rounded down, capped at 5,000,000 gp, the same rule the game uses. It's taken out of every margin, profit and ROI figure on the site, so what you read is what actually lands in your coin pouch.

I play several accounts. Do they get mixed together?

No. Each character the plugin reports is kept separate, and the switcher in the header picks which one you're looking at. Manual trades can be attributed to a character you name yourself on your profile. A savings goal is deliberately the exception: it counts everything, because it's a target for you, not for one account.

What happens if I lose my recovery code?

The account is gone. Only a hash of the code is stored, which is what lets the account work with no email address behind it, and why nothing here can look one up. Sign in with Discord if you'd rather not keep track of a code.

Can I use this alongside the desktop app?

Yes. Download it, then connect it from your profile under Desktop app and paste the token into Settings → Website. The two then share one journal: a trade recorded in either reaches the other within a minute.

Can I get my data out?

Yes, any time: export your journal as CSV. It's the whole thing, tracked positions and manual trades both, not just whatever table you happen to be looking at.

Can this get me banned?

It doesn't play the game, send input, or read anything the game hasn't already shown you. The plugin runs inside RuneLite, which Jagex permits, and reports events after they happen. More on that.
