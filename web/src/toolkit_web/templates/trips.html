How the numbers work. Supplies are the items that were in your inventory, equipment and bank at the last bank visit before the trip and were gone at the first one after it, priced as they were then. That counts anything that left your account, so something you sold or traded in the same window shows up there too. A trip without a bank visit on both sides shows loot only. Deaths are counted but not subtracted a second time, because what you lost is already part of what left.
